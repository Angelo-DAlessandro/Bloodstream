About the science
Bloodstream is an educational arcade interpretation of red-cell physiology. Core ideas are biologically grounded: erythroid maturation, deformability, pulmonary oxygen loading, tissue unloading, oxidative and osmotic stress, splenic filtration and erythrophagocytic risk, cold-storage stress, and transfusion into a recipient. Timing, scale, steering, and cell behaviors are deliberately stylized for play.

Explore the biology

Bone marrowErythroid maturation culminates in enucleation, reticulocyte maturation, and entry into the circulation through marrow sinusoids.
HeartRed cells are passive passengers in pulsatile flow. The game turns pressure waves and valves into timing challenges.
LungsHemoglobin oxygen saturation rises across pulmonary capillaries while RBCs deform through vessels narrower than their resting diameter.
Systemic organsOxygen unloading depends on local demand, pH, CO₂, temperature, and hemoglobin affinity. Here it becomes a delivery mechanic.
SpleenSplenic filtration tests deformability and membrane health. Damaged or senescent cells are more likely to be retained and cleared.
Blood storageCold storage produces metabolic, redox, and membrane changes collectively described as the storage lesion.
TransfusionStored donor RBCs return to circulation in a recipient, where successful survival and oxygen delivery complete the mission.
Game abstractionScale, time, anatomy, steering, hazards, and encounter frequency are intentionally stylized. This is not a quantitative simulator.
