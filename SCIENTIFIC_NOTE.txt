BLOODSTREAM — SCIENTIFIC DESIGN NOTE

Bloodstream is intended as a playful, accessible representation of red blood cell biology and transfusion medicine.

Biologically grounded concepts represented in the current prototype:
• erythroid maturation and marrow egress
• red-cell deformability
• pulmonary oxygen loading
• systemic oxygen unloading
• pulsatile cardiac flow
• oxidative stress
• osmotic stress
• microvascular constraint
• splenic filtration
• erythrophagocytic risk
• inflammatory / complement-like hazards
• cold-storage stress
• additive-solution support
• transfusion and post-transfusion oxygen delivery

Deliberate abstractions:
• organ geometry is schematic
• physiological time is compressed
• individual red cells do not actively steer in vivo
• hazards are exaggerated or spatially stylized
• immune and drug interactions are simplified
• scoring and energy mechanics are game systems, not physiological measurements

The objective is not to simulate circulation quantitatively, but to make core red-cell concepts intuitive and memorable.
